Self-hosted Outfit and Noto Sans SC variable fonts (weights 100–900).
Source: Google Fonts CSS API, downloaded 2026-11-13.
Licenses: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see the included OFL files.
fonts.css preserves the upstream Unicode ranges, so browsers download only
the subsets needed by the displayed text. No Google Fonts requests are made
by the website. Full upstream subsets are retained for future content edits.
Noto Sans SC is the Simplified Chinese member of the Noto CJK family.
